# Pricing Policy

The demo parking lot charges by the started hour. Standard parking is INR 40 per started hour. EV charging parking is INR 60 per started hour while the vehicle is connected to a charger. Accessible parking follows the standard parking rate.

Reservations have no extra fee in this POC. A production system should add a payment service and an auditable billing ledger rather than allowing an LLM to calculate or authorize payments directly.

Peak hours are 09:00-13:00 and 17:00-21:00 on weekdays. The POC does not dynamically change prices during peak hours; this is a policy reference for future demand-based pricing.
